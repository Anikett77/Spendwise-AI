## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no account, no catch. SpendWise is a free tool. We make money when users with significant savings opportunity choose to explore Credex's discounted AI credits — but that's always optional, never required to see your results.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No. You land on the page, enter your tools, and get your results. Email is optional and only asked after your results are shown — not before.

**Q: How accurate are the savings estimates?**
A: Every recommendation traces to a specific vendor pricing page (see PRICING_DATA.md). We check prices weekly. The logic is rule-based and deterministic — not AI guessing. A finance person can verify every number.

**Q: Will you share my company's spending data?**
A: No. The shareable result URL contains your tool selections and savings figures, but identifying information (email, company name) is never in the public URL. We don't sell data.

**Q: What AI tools do you cover?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude (all plans including API), ChatGPT (all plans including API), Anthropic API direct, OpenAI API direct, Gemini, and Windsurf. We add tools as they become significant spend items for startups.
