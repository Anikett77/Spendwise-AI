# Reflection

> ⚠️ IMPORTANT — These 5 questions must be answered in your own words.
> 150–400 words each. Specific > general. Name actual files, bugs, tools, decisions.
> Credex reads this carefully. Vague answers score poorly.

---

## 1. The Hardest Bug You Hit This Week

*Describe a specific bug: what was the symptom, what hypotheses did you form, what did you try, what actually fixed it?*

**Example structure (replace with your real experience):**
On Day [X], [what broke]. The symptom was [specific error or behavior]. My first hypothesis was [X] because [reason]. I tried [specific thing] — that didn't work because [reason]. Then I noticed [clue] in [file/console/log]. The actual cause was [root cause]. Fixed by [specific change]. The lesson: [what you'd do differently].

---

## 2. A Decision You Reversed Mid-Week

*What did you decide early on, why did you reverse it, and what made you change your mind?*

**Example structure:**
Initially I decided to [X] because [reason]. By Day [X] I reversed this because [what changed — new information, a bug, user feedback, rethinking the requirements]. The reversal cost [X hours] but was worth it because [specific outcome].
